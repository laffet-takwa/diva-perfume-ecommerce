## Important — check before going live

- `chanel-no-5.jpg` is openly licensed (CC BY 2.0, via Flickr, credited in
  `../perfumes/CREDITS.md` lineage) and safe to use with attribution.
- The remaining seven are **retail product photographs of branded packaging**.
  They were sourced for this prototype as visual references. Brand names,
  packaging and bottle shapes are trademarks and copyright of their respective
  owners, and none of these files carry a commercial-use licence.
- Before this storefront takes real orders, replace them with imagery you are
  licensed to sell — official press assets from each brand's trade portal, your
  distributor's product feed, or commissioned photography against a plain
  backdrop. Drop-in replacement only needs the same filename in this folder.

Fragrance names, houses and note pyramids in the catalogue are factual
descriptions of real products. Prices are indicative and in DT.
